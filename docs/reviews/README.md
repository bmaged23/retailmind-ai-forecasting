# Product feature review

Source descriptions are from UCI Online Retail II (Chen, 2012, DOI 10.24432/C5CG6D, CC BY 4.0). `pack-review-sample.csv` contains a reproducible 60-description sample selected from the pre-cutoff catalogue: 20 explicitly marked set/pack, 20 with shorthand or leading numbers, and 20 other descriptions. Within each stratum, 10 were assigned to development and 10 to held-out review using seed 142. The strata are intentionally enriched for challenging cases and are **not representative** of all descriptions.

The development annotations were completed before the held-out descriptions were reviewed; the updated rule was frozen before held-out annotation. Both sets were reviewed by the same assistant and have not been independently adjudicated. Labels distinguish explicit set/pack counts, no explicit count and ambiguous numeric phrases. Ambiguous cases are not treated as negatives when calculating false positives.

The evaluation in `scripts/evaluate_pack_review.py` found, for each 30-row split: 13 explicit counts correctly extracted, nine no-explicit-count examples with no extracted count, and eight ambiguous cases where the extractor abstained. These numbers demonstrate behavior on this small, targeted sample, not dataset-wide precision or recall. Labels and sampling criteria are partly aligned with the explicit-phrase rule; obtain an independent, representative labeled set before claiming reliable product taxonomy or pack-count accuracy. Leading numbers like `5 HOOK HANGER` may denote item attributes; the parser leaves them unknown. Any inferred pack size is based on a title and may not be the true sales-unit pack size.

`code-family-sample.csv` contains 15 seed-selected repeated numeric bases with nonempty descriptions available before the cutoff. `code-family-review.csv` records a qualitative single-reviewer assessment: 13 appear related, two are uncertain. In one group, `84520B` and `84520b` have matching descriptions; this may be a case variant, but there is no proof that the records are interchangeable. Never merge solely based on this sample. Code-family grouping is an unverified candidate, not a manufacturer or product-quality label.

## Additional random holdout

After reviewing a further 40 randomly selected titles as development data, the parser was extended to recognize spelled-out counts such as `SET OF THREE`. Then 50 other descriptions were sampled at random (seed 4320) from the remaining products with known descriptions; the parser was fixed before these titles were reviewed. One assistant labeled the titles without independent adjudication. Eleven have an explicit count: ten were correctly extracted and one, `EIGHT PIECE SNAKE SET`, was missed. The rule produced no count for the 36 labeled negatives or three ambiguous cases. The sample is small, excludes earlier reviewed products and is conditional on descriptions existing at the cutoff, so these findings are not a guarantee of overall extraction accuracy. `pack-random-heldout-labels.csv` and `scripts/evaluate_pack_review.py` preserve the examples and counts.
