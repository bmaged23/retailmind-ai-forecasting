# Data validation and proposed benchmark

The validation script is reproducible with `uv run python scripts/validate_data.py` after preparing the workbook. It creates `reports/generated/data-validation.json` and a cohort CSV. `scripts/audit_workbook.py` separately inspects excluded codes and extreme transactions. These outputs are local generated artifacts.

The prepared panel includes every calendar date from 2009-12-01 to 2011-12-09 at least once. This is a dataset-wide presence check, not proof of source completeness or product availability. Positive SKU-day sales have median 6 units, 99th percentile 225, and maximum 80,995. The two largest positive transactions, 80,995 and 74,215 units, are each followed minutes later by a same-SKU, same-magnitude negative cancellation entry. This is evidence of likely reversals, though the current audit does not prove invoice-level matching. Gross sales currently include the positive lines and exclude their negatives, so these spikes can materially distort results. No automatic matching or clipping has yet been applied; develop and validate a cancellation-matching policy and report gross versus adjusted sensitivity before benchmarking. The original letter-prefix filter wrongly excluded products including DCGS0058 (MISO PRETTY GUM), DCGSSGIRL (GIRLS PARTY BAG) and DCGS0076 (SUNJAR LED NIGHT LIGHT). The updated policy includes the DCGS family and PADS and retains other letter-prefixed codes in a service/unclassified bucket; genuine services include POST and DOT. This is a conservative, incomplete review: other alphabetic codes may still be products, and numeric codes may also include non-products.

## Proposed protocol

Configuration is in `configs/benchmark.json`. Select up to 100 products on 2011-04-30 using only sales observed by then: a first recorded sale by the start of the 90-day lookback, at least 14 sale days during the lookback, and a sale in the last 28 days. Rank by gross units during the lookback, breaking ties by code. This initial active-product cohort is not representative of all or intermittent products; a separately defined intermittent cohort is needed. Zero rows based on a product's later last-sale date are not used to decide cohort membership. A test confirms future observations do not affect selection.

The proposed cutoffs are validation 2011-05-31 and 2011-06-28; calibration 2011-07-26; test 2011-08-23, 2011-09-20 and 2011-10-18. Each predicts the following 28 days, with 90-day context. These forecast target periods do not overlap. Select models and settings using validation, calibrate using calibration, and perform final comparisons on test. Later forecast origins may use observations available at their respective cutoffs, but model selection and calibration choices must remain fixed; distinguish fixed weights from refitting at each cutoff.

Of the 100 selected SKUs, target windows are complete for both validation cutoffs, calibration and the first test cutoff, but only 99 and 97 for the last two test cutoffs. This is a consequence of stopping the panel at each SKU's last recorded sale; availability is unknown. Report coverage and missing targets by model and fold, and use a common comparison mask without changing the cohort after observing future outcomes. Do not silently convert unobserved post-last-sale periods to zeros. The existing `retailmind backtest` still selects products using future coverage and must be replaced before it can implement this protocol.

## Compute constraint

The user requested at most 2 GB GPU VRAM for **both training and inference**. Enforce a cap for frameworks that support it, account for non-framework allocations and other processes, measure peak total device memory, and avoid a GPU execution if the limit cannot be guaranteed. Use CPU or mark the experiment infeasible instead of exceeding the budget. GPU visibility was blocked in the initial environment check; verify access and headroom before any GPU experiment. Report device, peak memory and runtime for each run.

The full-history dashboard was created before these dates were chosen. It is exploratory, and therefore the held-out periods are not entirely blind to human inspection. Disclose this when reporting results and consider an additional untouched dataset or future period for a truly blind assessment.
