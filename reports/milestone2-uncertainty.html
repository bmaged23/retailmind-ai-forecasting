Method and limits

Base P10/P50/P90 quantiles come from the saved Timestral LoRA adapter. Horizon-specific conformal quantile adjustments are estimated from August residuals; positive adjustments widen intervals and negative adjustments narrow them. Corrections are frozen before later evaluation labels are scored.

This historical evaluation is diagnostic, not a pristine blind benchmark. Earlier project work examined some dates and product subsets in these periods. SKU and date dependence also weakens formal conformal guarantees. Forecasts model recorded sales; stockouts and product discontinuations are not observed.
