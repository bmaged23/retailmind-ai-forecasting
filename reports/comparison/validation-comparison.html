Protocol and interpretation

Fit targets end 2011-04-30. Checkpoint validation uses 2011-05-01 through 2011-05-28. All models forecast from 2011-06-30 over the same 4,366 products and 28 target dates. July labels were loaded only after every forecast passed exact key checks.

Missing transaction dates are scored as zero recorded sales; this does not distinguish no demand from unavailable or discontinued products. The October split is excluded from model selection because it was previously reused.
