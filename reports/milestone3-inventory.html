Scenario assumptions

Initial stock: uniform 3–14 days of 90-day average sales
Supplier lead time: uniform 2–10 days
Existing in-transit order: 25% chance; 0.5–1.5 weeks of average sales
Review period: 7 days; one new order at the forecast origin
Holding: £0.05/unit/day; order: £5.00
Shortage penalty: £2.00/unmet unit; seed 42

Interpretation and limits

The Timestral policy sets target stock to forecast P50 demand over lead time plus the seven-day review period, with the sum of daily P90–P50 gaps as a safety-stock heuristic. The comparator repeats the last seven observed daily sales and orders up to that expected demand without a safety buffer. Both share the same starting stock, outstanding order, lead time, recorded-sales path, and cost assumptions.

All stock, supplier, and cost inputs are simulated. The replay uses historical recorded sales as a demand proxy; it cannot reveal lost sales during stockouts. Summed daily P90 bounds are not calibrated aggregate quantiles. The policy places one order at the origin and does not reforecast during the 28-day replay. Treat the result as a policy prototype, not measured business savings.
